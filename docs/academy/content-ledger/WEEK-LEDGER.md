# Yomu Academy three-year week ledger

A three-year, Lesson-0-onward scaffold built on the strongest available evidence. YEAR 1 + YEAR 2 use the Genki Study Resources static site (lessons/lesson-0 .. lesson-23) as the digital backbone: Lesson 0 (writing system + greetings) through Lesson 12 = Genki I (Year 1, N5->N4), Lessons 13-23 = Genki II (Year 2, N4). These units are high-confidence in CONTENT (grammar points cite exact Genki page/problem numbers in the root index.html) but carry NO calendar dates — order is derived purely from the embedded lesson number. YEAR 3 is the only calendar-dated, real-classroom term captured: the class continues its own sequential 'Chapter' counter PAST Genki's 23 into Minna no Nihongo Shokyu II, and the captured sessions are Chapters 28-1 .. 30-2 (download batches 2026-02-17 / 03-04 / 03-10). Chapters 24-27 sit between the Genki backbone and the captured class term and are NOT present in any cluster: they are represented as low-confidence structural-bridge units only, with no invented contents. Duplicate dated lesson folders (Feb + Mar batches) are byte-identical re-downloads of the SAME session, so each class session is preserved as one unit (the duplication is recorded under supersession, not as separate weeks). Dates on Year-3 units are DOWNLOAD/batch dates, not confirmed class dates.

> Three-year chronological spine from Lesson 0. Every captured worksheet is preserved as a distinct occurrence; byte-identical re-download batches are retained as duplicate occurrences (see source ledger duplicate links). Un-captured structural bridge units (orders 24-27) are low-confidence placeholders derived from the class chapter counter, not from observed files.

**34 units** · **1053 assets placed in weeks** · 15 supporting dataset groups.
