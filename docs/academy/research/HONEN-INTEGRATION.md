# Honen augmentation contract

Honen is a source-indexing, drafting, revision, and instructor-workflow layer for Yomu Academy. It does not replace the Academy runtime or become a second learner experience.

## Ownership boundary

Honen may:

- index the complete Japanese source census and preserve stable source references;
- search, compare, quote, summarize, and cross-link exact source material;
- draft lesson structures and revisions;
- propose retrieval, interleaving, listening, speaking, writing, and transfer variants;
- produce instructor-facing source maps and review queues.

Yomu owns:

- canonical N+1 order and learner prerequisites;
- the visual-novel story, cast voices, relationships, locations, and pacing;
- SRS evidence and progression;
- minigame implementation and scoring;
- art, audio, music, SFX, accessibility, responsive layout, and offline behavior;
- route reachability, persistence, tests, release, and live proof.

## Knowledge-base structure

The Honen root is `Yomu Academy — Complete Japanese Corpus`.

1. `00 — Source Census & Provenance`
2. `01 — N5 Foundations`
3. `02 — N4 Elementary`
4. `03 — N3 Intermediate`
5. `04 — N2 Advanced`
6. `05 — N1 Mastery`
7. `06 — Listening, Audio & Pronunciation`
8. `07 — Dictionaries, Corpora & Reference`
9. `08 — Learning Science & Soya Research`
10. `09 — Yomu Story, Bible & Experience`
11. `10 — Course Drafts & Revision Queues`

The source census records all local files. Direct Honen import uses only file formats accepted by the active knowledge-base uploader. Unsupported formats remain first-class indexed sources and continue through Yomu's local source adapters.

## Course families

Each canonical lesson can request multiple Honen outputs without multiplying the learner-facing syllabus:

- teacher source brief;
- first encounter;
- guided recognition;
- constrained production;
- listening discrimination;
- speaking shadow and substitution;
- writing or handwriting;
- story-context transfer;
- retrieval repair;
- spaced revision;
- mixed cumulative check;
- optional challenge;
- instructor revision report.

Honen output is a proposal until it has exact source loci, prerequisite fit, answer gating, targeted repair, SRS semantics, Yomu narrative adaptation, and mobile/desktop route proof.

## Day 0 / Day 1 first slice

The first augmentation target is the Rie-led desk-language loop:

- `しゅくだい` is introduced on a real take-home paper;
- `れい` is introduced on a worked example;
- each word is heard and matched once;
- the papers swap position for transfer;
- a wrong answer repairs only the current word;
- the first successful retrieval seeds exact SRS evidence;
- kana is secondary and optional at this point;
- the next route recommends review but always offers `Continue anyway`.

Honen may propose alternate prop layouts, distractor rationales, delayed retrieval prompts, and instructor diagnostics. It must not add untaught Japanese, expose the answer before an attempt, turn the interaction into generic courseware, or replace Rie and the classroom story.
