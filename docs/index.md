## What It Does

よむ is a small helper that runs inside your browser. When you point it at Japanese text, subtitles, or manga images, it opens a clean popup with readings, meanings, mining actions, examples, kanji details, and audio.

It is meant for learners who want the useful parts of paid reading suites without turning study into another subscription. You can start with JPDB only, import local Yomitan dictionaries later, or connect Anki when you are ready.
