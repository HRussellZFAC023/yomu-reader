# Chapter 3 - 「いっしょに食べよう」 The Rain Page

**Core question:** A card says refreshments are available. Does it tell a first-time visitor that they are invited, what choices exist, or what happens if the weather changes?
**Story change:** The third Blue Door card becomes a real invitation, with options and an ordinary rain alternative.
**Language family:** `〜ませんか/〜ましょう`, `〜が好きです`, food and refreshment words, `〜つ/〜人/〜杯`, time and place review.
**Runtime status:** planned N5 sequence. The current Level 3+ meal-plan lesson is deliberately reserved for Chapter 5's N4 rehearsal.

## S3.1 - A Table Is Not Yet an Invitation

**Location:** Rain Window, L04.
**Narrative job:** Give Nori's welcoming instinct a practical language problem.
**Authentic language need:** Turn an announcement into a low-pressure invitation.
**Learning evidence:** `planned:activity-n5-invite-masenka` - ordering and `select-one` practice for `〜ませんか/〜ましょう`.
**Intent ID:** `table.invite-with-room-to-decline`

_The new card reads `お茶と 食べ物が あります`. Nori has placed a tiny tray beside it: two cups, one apple, and a napkin folded as if it has been given a job title._

**NORI:** `これは、やさしいです。でも、来ていいか 分かりますか。`
_This is kind. But does it tell someone they are allowed to come?_

**RIE:** `いいところに気づきました。「あります」は知らせる言葉です。今日は、誘う言葉も作りましょう。`
_You noticed something important. Arimasu tells someone a thing exists. Today we will also make language that invites._

**Explanation - use before label:**

> `お茶を 飲みませんか。` - Would you like some tea?
> `いっしょに 休みましょう。` - Let’s take a break together.

`〜ませんか` offers without demanding yes. Once a plan is shared, `〜ましょう` names the next step together.

**Variant spine - same intent:**

| N5                      | Bridge                              | N4                                           |
| ----------------------- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- |
| `お茶を 飲みませんか。` | `よかったら、お茶を 飲みませんか。` | `よかったら、少しお茶を飲んでいきませんか。` |

**Recap if skipped:** _The refreshments card needs an invitation, not only a list of things that exist._
**Handoff:** `planned:activity-n5-invite-masenka`.

## S3.2 - An Option Is a Welcome

**Location:** Card Rack and refreshment tray.
**Narrative job:** Give the group a way to ask about preference without asking for disclosure.
**Authentic language need:** Offer two ordinary choices and say what is available.
**Learning evidence:** `planned:activity-n5-preference-suki` - choose `が` with `好き`, form an invitation response.
**Intent ID:** `table.offer-preference-without-assumption`

_Suzu has written `ケーキ`. Nori adds `くだもの`. Suzu writes `ケーキ` again, slightly smaller. Nori waits._

**SUZU:** `ケーキが あります。これで いいですか。`
_There is cake. Is this enough?_

**NORI:** `好きな ものが ちがう人も います。聞けたら、やさしいです。`
_People may like different things. If we can ask, that is kinder._

**RIE:** `そうですね。「何が好きですか」は、答えを決めない聞き方です。`
_Exactly. “What do you like?” asks without deciding the answer._

**Teaching card:**

| Need             | Japanese                     |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------- |
| offer a choice   | `お茶と 水が あります。`     |
| ask preference   | `何が 好きですか。`          |
| state preference | `わたしは お茶が 好きです。` |
| keep it open     | `どちらでも 大丈夫です。`    |

**Practice prompt:** Complete: `わたしは 水 ___ 好きです。` -> `が`.

**Recap if skipped:** _A welcome does not assume one right choice. It names options and leaves room to choose._
**Handoff:** `planned:activity-n5-preference-suki`.

## S3.3 - Count What a Reader Can See

**Location:** Refreshment table.
**Narrative job:** Make quantities useful for a small shared plan rather than a detached drill.
**Authentic language need:** Say what is on the table and how many people it serves.
**Learning evidence:** `planned:activity-n5-table-counters` - matching and ordering for `つ/人/杯`.
**Intent ID:** `table.count-visible-options`

_Leo counts the cups. He counts them again because a third cup has appeared. Rie looks toward the kettle. It looks innocent._

**LEO:** `お茶は 三杯です。人は 四人です。`
_There are three cups of tea. There are four people._

**RIE:** `では、カードには何を書きますか。`
_Then what shall we write on the card?_

**NORI:** `お茶を、もう一杯 作りましょう。`
_Let’s make one more cup of tea._

**Teaching card:**

| What is counted | Counter | Example         |
| --------------- | ------- | --------------- |
| general items   | `〜つ`  | `りんごを 二つ` |
| people          | `〜人`  | `四人`          |
| cups/bowls      | `〜杯`  | `お茶を 三杯`   |

**Practice prompt:** A card has four guests and three drinks. Choose the useful next sentence: `お茶を もう一杯 作りましょう。`

**Recap if skipped:** _Counting becomes useful when it tells the next person what needs to happen next._
**Handoff:** `planned:activity-n5-table-counters`.

## S3.4 - The Rain Icon Needs Words

**Location:** Rain Window.
**Narrative job:** Introduce a weather alternative without jumping ahead to Chapter 5 condition grammar.
**Authentic language need:** Say where the table moves in ordinary rain.
**Learning evidence:** `planned:activity-n5-table-plan-listening` - listen/read for time, place, and alternate location.
**Intent ID:** `table.name-rain-alternative`

_At the bottom of the card is a small umbrella icon. It is beautiful and entirely unhelpful. Mika places a dry chair under the window, then points at the icon._

**MIKA:** `雨の ときは、どこですか。`
_When it rains, where is it?_

**RIE:** `そうです。絵は気づかせます。文は動けるようにします。`
_Yes. A picture helps us notice. A sentence lets us act._

**N5 card:**

> `雨の ときは、カフェです。デスクの 人に 聞いて ください。`
> _When it rains, use the cafe. Ask the Desk person._

**Bridge support:** `雨が 降ったら、カフェで 休みましょう。` appears as a preview only; `〜たら` is taught and assessed later in Chapter 5.

**Variant spine - same immediate intent:**

| N5                          | Bridge                                   | N4                                             |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| `雨の ときは、カフェです。` | `雨が 降ったら、カフェで 休みましょう。` | `雨なら、ぬれないようにカフェに変えませんか。` |

**Recap if skipped:** _The rain icon gained an indoor place and a person who can clarify the next step._
**Handoff:** `planned:activity-n5-table-plan-listening`.

## S3.5 - Optional Study Connection: Window Rehearsal, First Version

**Location:** Rain Window.
**Narrative job:** Let Nori test an invitation through text, where declining remains ordinary.
**Authentic language need:** Rehearse an invitation and a no-pressure response.
**Learning evidence:** `planned:activity-n5-invitation-message`; no recording required.
**Intent ID:** `nori.rehearse-invitation-with-choice`

_Nori reads the draft to the empty chair by the window. Then stops before the last line._

**NORI:** `「来ませんか」の あとに、「だめでも 大丈夫です」を 書きたいです。`
_After “would you like to come?”, I want to write “it is alright if not.”_

**RIE:** `書いてもいいです。ただ、短い形でも、断る場所はあります。`
_You may write that. The short form already leaves room to decline, too._

**Learner choice - cosmetic:**

- `よかったら、お茶を 飲みませんか。`
- `お茶を 飲みませんか。`

_Both become alternate caption variants and rejoin at the same practice task._

**Connection recap:** _An invitation can be warm without explaining away every possible answer._
**Optional payoff:** `Window Rehearsal: invitation` recap card.

## S3.6 - The Rain Page Is Ready

**Location:** Card Rack, chapter close.
**Narrative job:** Create a card that says welcome in operational language.
**Authentic language need:** Write a short invitation with a choice, time/place, and rain alternative.
**Learning evidence:** `planned:activity-n5-write-invitation` - long-text self-review with model and three-criterion rubric.
**Intent ID:** `folio.publish-rain-page`

_The revised card is plain enough to be used and warm enough to be read twice._

> `六時に、ゴードン・スクエアで お茶を 飲みませんか。お茶と 水が あります。雨の ときは、カフェです。デスクの 人に 聞いて ください。`

**SUZU:** `これなら、来る人は「いいですか」と聞かなくてもいいです。`
_With this, a person arriving does not need to ask whether they are allowed._

**NORI:** `聞いても いいです。でも、聞かなくても 来られるのが いいです。`
_They are allowed to ask. But it is good if they can arrive without having to._

_Inside the fold is the original author’s pencilled note: `食べ物だけでは、席にならない。` - “Food alone does not make a place at the table.” It adds evidence to the folio’s purpose without turning the writer into a suspect._

**Model answer - after first attempt:**

> `六時に、広場で お茶を 飲みませんか。水も あります。雨の ときは、カフェです。わからない ときは、デスクに 聞いて ください。`

**Self-review:** Can a reader identify the invitation, options, time/place, and rain alternative?
**Chapter recap:**

- `〜ませんか` invites without requiring yes; `〜ましょう` names a shared next step.
- `〜が好きです` helps a reader express preference; a welcome offers choices before asking for an answer.
- Counters make a small shared plan visible.
- A weather icon is not a fallback until it names what to do.

**Handoff:** Campus; Chapter 4 quiet-room task; **Continue learning** opens the next unfinished activity.

## Chapter 3 author notes

- Food is functional hospitality, not a restaurant fantasy or a test of taste. Use ordinary, inclusive examples and never require dietary disclosure.
- Nori's flaw is over-accommodation, not shyness. Their arc is learning that a clear invitation can be kinder than an endlessly padded one.
- Chapter 3 may preview `たら` in a clearly marked bridge layer, but it does not assess it before Chapter 5.
- The completed Rain Page is a recap item. It does not unlock food content, routes, or access settings.
