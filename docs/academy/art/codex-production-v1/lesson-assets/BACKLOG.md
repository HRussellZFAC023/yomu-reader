## Non-Negotiable Gates

- No generated readable text, signage, map labels, worksheet copy, trademarks, watermarks, or pseudo-type.
- No source-image copying from textbook, Moodle, Soya, or other audit-only collections without recorded rights clearance.
- No wide scene can be marked full-bleed mobile without an authored mobile companion.
- Every character scene uses a single identity once in the composed frame; baked CG and a second sprite never coexist.
- Every final needs a 200% close inspection and real target-viewport review before release.
