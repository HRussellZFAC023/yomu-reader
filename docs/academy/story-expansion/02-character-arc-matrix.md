# Character Arc Matrix

Who each person is across the term, and how the room warms up around them. Nobody has a
biography — everyone has a small want and a hobby they love. The grammar picks the person
(spine §5); this doc just makes sure each person is somewhere, wanting something, twice or
more. Canon order: SPINE.md, STORY-BIBLE.md, then this. Scene ids are fixed by
`01-scene-graph.md`; task and `ext-*` ids are the real ones from `cast-learning.ts`.

Two things this doc is careful about:

- **Nobody becomes a joke for a language mistake.** Gags are about cars, recorders, and
  spreadsheets — never about someone getting a particle wrong. Mika is shy, not slow.
- **The whole cast-learning rule is provable here.** Every classmate speaks in **≥2 route
  stages** — the proof table at the end cites the exact tasks.

---

## How to read a person

Each block gives:

- **Wants** — present tense, human-sized, no backstory. What they'd like tonight.
- **Arc** — a beginning, a small turn, a payoff. Modest by design; this is a night class,
  not an opera.
- **Anchors** — the real scenes they carry, with the `cast-learning.ts` task each frames.
- **Rel-deltas** — the optional, cosmetic Study Connection (`coursemate → acquainted →
  friend → close`, spine §7b) mirrored at real scenes. These are texture the story
  *reflects*; the connection itself is hide-able and costs nothing to skip.
- **Gag** — the running joke and when it comes back.
- **Extension** — their one `ext-<id>` task: its `unlockAfterRoute`, and the Study-Link step
  that surfaces it. The full wiring lives in `03-relationship-unlock-table.md`; this is the
  per-person view.

**Two warmth layers, kept separate.** The *class* warms in one direction across the whole
term (`strangers → a-room-of-people → regulars → a-family-forming → a-team-with-a-plan →
friends-who-travelled`, spine §7a — global texture, the scene graph's rel-state column).
A *person's* Study Connection is a private, optional four-step thing. Don't confuse them:
the room can be `regulars` while your bond with Shin is still `coursemate`, and that's fine.

## The two who carry weight

Alex and Jodi get the deepest treatment: Alex's job is the turn the whole term pivots on
(C5.7), and Jodi's Tokyo is the ember that lit the trip (C4.4).

### Alex · `alex` — the quiet senpai who says little and means all of it

- **Wants:** to keep things low-key. Would much rather help than be helped. Climbs
  mountains and mentions it like a weekend errand.
- **Arc.** *Beginning:* he's the calm one in the corner — steady at the kana check (PR.5,
  `f0-kana-check`), and later the person who meets Stasi's plan halfway with a reason the
  whole group can live with (C5.4, `l6-library-choice`). He teaches the class how to take
  one step at a time; his whole register is *understatement*. *The turn (C5.7, EV-joboffer):*
  Alex mentions he's moving to Japan for work like it's nothing — and for once the class
  won't let him wave it off. The room's reaction **is** the scene. The senpai who never asks
  for anything is about to be the one who's sent off. *Payoff:* the surprise party (C6.3,
  everyone writes him one line in Japanese — the N4 payoff), the platform goodbye (C6.4),
  and then — Japan (C6.5), where he's not the one helping for once, he's the reason they all
  came. The quiet man finally lets the class do something for him.
- **Register for the turn (indicative — `09-special-scene-scripts.md` owns the final line):**
  he undersells it. Something like 「あのさ…来月、日本で働くことになったんだ。」 *"So… next
  month, turns out I'll be working over there."* No fuss. The fuss is everyone else's.
- **Anchors:** PR.5 (`f0-kana-check`), C5.4 (`l6-library-choice`), C5.7 (EV-joboffer,
  narrative), C6.3 (EV-party), C6.4 (station), C6.5 (EV-trip).
- **Rel-deltas:** `coursemate` (PR.5) → `acquainted` (C5.4, first real back-and-forth) →
  `friend` (C5.7, the room rallies) → `close` (C6.3/C6.4, the send-off).
- **Gag:** not a comic gag — a quiet motif. He mentions enormous things (Fuji, a new life
  abroad) in the flattest possible voice. The class learns to listen for what he *doesn't*
  stress. `for-work-someday` reason-token callback lands on him at the send-off (spine §6).
- **Extension:** `ext-alex-route-memory` · unlockAfterRoute **6** · N4 · surfaces at
  **SL-alex.3 "The summit"** (sequencing + past experience). "I checked the map, then
  walked. Long, but quiet." — sequencing calm, which is exactly how he takes the biggest
  step of the term.

### Jodi · `jodi` — the one who's been there, and the class's quiet heart

- **Wants:** for the class to *feel* the Tokyo she remembers, without the evening becoming
  about her. Studies hardest of anyone — she's chasing a memory, not a grade.
- **Arc.** *Beginning:* warm and easy in the introduction circle (C1.2,
  `l1-introduction-handoff`) — present, never performing. *The turn (C4.4, EV-tokyo-ember):*
  one softer evening, Jodi shows a few photos of a Tokyo that half-exists now, the room goes
  quiet in the good way, and someone finally says out loud, *"we should go."* She doesn't
  push it — she just lights it. *Payoff:* her memory turns practical when it counts — at
  trip prep she's the one who quietly volunteers to check the ticket times (C5.8,
  `l8-trip-preparation`) — and then she's actually *there*, on the temple street at night
  (C6.5), standing in the place she carried for years, now with a whole class around her.
- **Anchors:** C1.2 (`l1-introduction-handoff`), C4.4 (EV-tokyo-ember, narrative),
  C5.8 (`l8-trip-preparation`), C6.5 (EV-trip).
- **Rel-deltas:** `coursemate` (C1.2) → `acquainted` (C4.4, she opens the photos) →
  `friend` (C5.8, she trusts the class with the real logistics) → `close` (C6.5, the trip).
- **Gag:** gentle, not comic — 「昔ね、日本に住んでいたの。」 *"You know, I used to live in
  Japan."* It stops being a wistful aside and becomes a plan. If `pr.reason = for-a-person`,
  she gives the temple-street callback 「その人に、これ、話してあげてね。」 (spine §6).
- **Extension:** `ext-jodi-small-memory` · unlockAfterRoute **8** · N4 · surfaces at
  **SL-jodi.3 "The Tokyo she knew"** (past tense + memory). "I got lost at this station
  before. Next time I'll check the map." — memory *becoming* preparation, her arc in one
  line.

## The sensei

### Rie · `rie` — your sensei, and somehow everywhere

- **Wants:** everyone to leave a little lighter than they came in. To keep all nine plates
  spinning without dropping a single student.
- **Arc.** Rie's arc isn't change — it's *constancy that keeps paying off*. She saved you a
  seat before you arrived (PR.1). Her small turn is C1.5, the pub: she turns up, orders,
  and turns out to be the funniest person in the room — the class learns the teacher is a
  person. After that the running gag does the emotional work: you keep bumping into her
  (the midnight konbini till, C2.4; the station kiosk at the goodbye, C6.4), always
  delighted, always exhausted, always kind. Payoff is C6.7 — she opens the next story the
  way she opened this one.
- **Anchors:** every route stage. PR.4 (`f0-classroom-repair`), C1.1 (`l1-likes-circle`),
  C2.1 (`l2-find-the-cafe`), C3.1 (`l3-food-invitation`), C4.1 (`l4-weekend-recall`),
  C5.1 (`l5-gentle-study-advice`), C5.3 (`l6-cafe-reasons`), C5.5 (`l7-classroom-incident`),
  C5.8 (`l8-trip-preparation`), C6.1 (`l9-inclusive-restaurant-plan`) — plus the narrative
  set-pieces PR.1–PR.3, C2.4, C5.R6, C5.11, C6.4, C6.7.
- **Rel-deltas:** she starts closer than anyone (she remembers what each student is scared
  of) — treat her Study Connection as `acquainted` from the pub onward; `close` at
  SL-rie.3, when marking papers together stops feeling like office hours.
- **Gag:** nine jobs (konbini C2.4 → station C6.4 is the setup/callback spine); the dead
  marker that gives up on 「どこ」 again; the hand-drawn 花丸. She teases, never scolds.
- **Extension:** `ext-rie-office-hour` · unlockAfterRoute **5** · N4 · surfaces at
  **SL-rie.3 "Marking papers together"** (reasons + encouragement). "You don't have to fix
  everything. First, pick the one thing you want to say." — the whole Academy in one breath.
