# Deep Character Dossier — Jenny

**Canonical role:** lead of `s1e12-the-vanishing-course`; class-continuity beats at chapters 12 / 13 / 29 / 44 / 48.
**Voice anchor (canon):** composed, sensory, and economical; asks only after trying privately. Chooses when to step back and returns on terms the group has heard. Learning/callback home: offers, description, work language; knitting used as practical repair.
**Contrast partner (canon):** Christian — both solve practical problems; Christian acts outwardly, Jenny tries privately before asking. Christian uses imperative sequences; Jenny uses economical sensory observations and one precise request.
**Pressure rule (canon):** under stress Jenny goes quiet and attempts the task alone; growth is naming the role she *does* want, not becoming publicly extroverted.
**Status:** `continuity-only` by default. The elective route in §4 is a *proposal*; it is not playable until a reviewed `RelationshipManifestEntry` sets `status: bond-authored` with six ordered packages.

## 1. Who they really are

Jenny is the warm gravitational centre of the class who almost never needs the spotlight to hold it. In the real chat she introduced herself as "the one with たくさん物" — too much stuff — and that self-deprecating shrug is the whole voice in miniature: she carries more than she lets on, and she'd rather the room laugh with her than clock how much she's actually holding up. She is the most prolific poster, the chief sharer of reels and resources and photos of cute food, and the first to say *get better soon* when someone drops out. But — and this is the reconciliation the canon voice anchor demands — her warmth is **economical and private-first**, not performative. She reads a room in one glance, notices who's gone quiet, and has a kind word ready. She does not host loudly. She tries the thing alone, and only then asks one precise question. The prolific-poster energy and the composed-quiet-noticer are the same person: she puts effort in *before* it's visible, and the visible part is generous and light.

Her hands are always busy — there's a perpetual half-finished scarf. Knitting is her canon prop and her canon repair mechanism: when a plan tears, Jenny is the one who quietly re-threads it rather than announcing a fix. She wants to make wool jumpers "that will cost me hundreds," and she is proudly, comically **stingy with big purchases while spending freely on wool** — a frugality she shares in teasing solidarity with Aakash (*アカシさん、たくさん車がほしくないですか？🤪*). She has a long-term partner (male) who is a recurring warm presence in her orbit: he cooks for her, made the octopus-wiener bento, defrosts steak, took her for sukiyaki, and — the running joke — "can eat his bodyweight in gyoza." She does up a flat with him. She likes music (Stick Season; Shaboozey's *Tipsy*) and traded songs with Stasi; she got the *ラーメン大好き小泉さん* theme stuck in her head and loves *Full Metal Alchemist*. Her employer reimbursed her first term of classes, so she is, quietly, the classmate learning on the job. On the tech side she's an Android user who couldn't easily run Tampermonkey — the real thread where Henry patiently set her up with jpdb — which maps cleanly onto her being the person Henry's tool-lane *helps* rather than a tool person herself.

Her Japanese is the class's best portrait of an affectionate self-learner: she posts real casual Japanese, flags her own mistakes and typos ("used を instead of と"), asks genuinely good nuance questions, draws kanji in her phone's note app, and builds "fun" sentences with Google Translate + ChatGPT before breaking them into mini-lessons for everyone. Anki is her personified tormentor — *"My Anki is giving me bombastic side eye," "Anki is doing me dirty 🥲"* — a Minna-no-Nihongo deck she loves and neglects in equal measure and confesses to constantly. That love-hate confession is important: she fusses over her own study lapses without ever making anyone else feel behind.

*(Art note carried for the sprite team, not story canon: hair darker in v3; keep the perpetual scarf/knitting as her unique prop.)*

**Reconciliation with the anchor:** every real detail deepens "composed, sensory, economical; asks after trying privately; knitting as repair; offers/description/work language." Nothing here should tip her into a bustling extrovert host — that would violate the pressure rule. Her tea-and-kind-word instinct is real texture, but note the plot-prop boundary in §5: **the cold-tea callback belongs to Rie**, not Jenny. Jenny's owned prop is the scarf.

## 2. Four-season arc (her five continuity beats)

Her deeper arc, per the bible: *a bounded competence enters the shared work; a fictional rupture tests whether the class can support her without taking over; she returns on terms she chooses.* The cause of the rupture is invented; the emotion is real. Stepping back is a **complete choice, not a confidence deficit** (consent note, canon).

- **Ch 12 — Arrival — LEAD, `s1e12-the-vanishing-course`.** The evening's serving/hosting plan for a lantern-atlas route loses every cue — labels, counts, the order things should come out. Jenny, mid-scarf-row, doesn't try to rescue it solo forever; her growth-in-action is that she **asks the class into the scene**. She describes precisely what is still present and asks how many of each thing are actually needed. This is the *first ensemble callback payoff* of `callback:first-lantern`: her failed solo becomes an ensemble save that restores a *short* route, not the whole atlas. Seed of her personal knitting-repair prop: she re-threads one literal tangled cue while the class rebuilds around her. End state of Season 1 rests on exactly this — an ensemble success with the exhibition route still incomplete.

- **Ch 13 — Contribution — `s1e13-dinner-by-if` (Robert leads).** A surprise ingredient breaks Robert's perfect conditional dinner sequence (`callback:overbuilt-schedule` echo). Jenny's *bounded competence alters the shared work*: instead of proposing a whole new plan, she negotiates frequency and duration and **compares two options** so the group can accept a changed plan without anyone losing face. Real texture: her partner's gyoza-devouring capacity and her own cute-bento fluency make her the credible voice on "how much do we actually need / how often." Knitting-repair echoes as a *useful action*: she quietly fixes the seating/serving order the way she'd pick up a dropped stitch.

- **Ch 29 — Limit — `s3e05-chair-not-reserved`.** Offered a visible, public event role, Jenny **declines** — 「今回は、裏のほうを手伝いたい。」 (*this time I'd like to help from the back*). The group's first instinct is to treat the gap as a problem to solve *for* her. The scene's job (and the transform of `callback:open-chair`): the class learns to **redesign around her stated limit without pursuing her, filling the seat, or asking her to justify it.** This is her arc's spine — support means hearing the boundary, not curing a confidence she never lost. The knitting-repair prop transforms under disagreement: the thing she chooses to mend is the *plan's shape*, not her own visibility. Sensitivity anchor: the rupture that pushes her to step back is a **fictional** N3-band pressure; keep the emotion real and the cause invented — no medical, financial, or relationship gossip.

- **Ch 44 — Return — `s4e08-last-revision` (Stasi/Ruparna/Xingyu lead).** The class makes the public version vivid without restoring withdrawn detail. Jenny **returns on terms the group has already heard**: a backstage/editing role, exactly the 裏方 she named at 29 — proof the ensemble *changed its behavior* rather than just apologised. Her knitting-as-repair pays off here as adaptation: she and Stasi (music buddies) trim and re-work the piece the way you re-block a finished panel, preserving a withheld passage's *place* without exposing its content. She is present, essential, and never dragged to the front.

- **Ch 48 — Future — `s4e12-next-page` (graduation).** Payoff of `callback:open-chair`: a blank page is offered under the class's permission terms, **with no person assigned to occupy it** — the institutional form of everything Jenny taught the class about leaving a role open. Her future beat leaves one next possibility open (the finished scarf handed on, a plan made without demanding certainty) without predicting the real person's life. In the graduation montage she is one of the few *shown* rather than roll-called; the journal records the rest.

## 3. The Japanese their story teaches — LEARNING INTEGRATION (most important)

**Jenny's learning speciality (canon):** *offers, description, and work language* — plus her real habit of **checking in with one gentle question before offering help**: 「今日、つかれてる？お茶、いる？」 (ext-jenny-offer-help, N4: "Ask one neutral question before offering help."). She is the class's model for **soft, low-pressure, other-directed Japanese**: the language of noticing someone, describing a situation economically, and offering without imposing. Her real behaviour — trying privately, then asking one precise thing — is *itself* a pragmatics lesson the learner absorbs by copying her.

Beat-by-beat, tied to the registered reservoir (`CONTENT-LINKAGE.md`):

- **Ch 12 (Arrival, LEAD) — reservoir `l1-l17`–`l1-l18`; transfer: "describe what is present and ask how many are needed in a failed service plan."**
  - **Functions the scene makes the learner NEED:** existence/inventory (あります／います), **counters + quantity questions** (いくつ・何〜・〜つ・〜個・〜人), **sensory description** of what's on the table (adjective description: あたたかい／つめたい, いいにおい, まだ足りない), and the **offer/request pair** (〜ましょうか／〜てくれませんか, 〜をお願いします).
  - **Activity types:** vocabulary (food/serving inventory) → grammar (counters + existence) → speaking/transfer (reconstruct a serving plan aloud from what remains). The source surface (the lost cue cards) disappears; the learner must *count and describe the real table* to rebuild the order.
  - **Why the real texture makes it authentic:** Jenny's actual fluency is **cute Japanese food and bento** — octopus wieners, gyoza, sukiyaki. A failed serving plan is the one crisis where her real knowledge and the exact grammar target (existence + counters + polite offers) are the *same thing*. The learner needs いくつ / 何個 not as a drill but to answer "how many do we actually plate?"

- **Ch 13 (Contribution) — reservoir `l1-l19`–`l1-l20`; transfer: "negotiate frequency/duration and compare options before accepting a changed plan."**
  - **Functions:** **frequency & duration** (よく・ときどき・たいてい・〜時間・〜回), **comparison** (〜より・〜のほうが・どちらが), and gentle proposal of a changed plan (〜ましょうか, 〜たらどう？).
  - **Activity types:** grammar (frequency/duration adverbials) + speaking (comparison-and-choose) + listening (hear a broken conditional and locate the one changed variable). Transfer: reorder the dinner around a surprise ingredient using comparison, not a rebuild.
  - **Why the real texture:** her partner "can eat his bodyweight in gyoza" and her frugal-except-wool economy make **quantity/frequency/how-much-do-we-need** her native register. Comparing two options politely (so no one loses face) is exactly how she really talks.

- **Ch 29 (Limit) — content family 28–30 (N3 conditions, refusal, role negotiation).**
  - **Functions the scene makes the learner NEED:** **indirect refusal / boundary language** (今回は…, 〜のはちょっと…, 遠慮します, 〜より裏のほうを手伝いたい), softened conditions (〜たら／〜ば), and **honouring a heard "no"** (了解, じゃあ〜してもらえる？). One clear "no" ends persuasion — the learner practises *accepting* a refusal, not overcoming it.
  - **Activity types:** listening (detect a softened refusal under polite surface) → speaking (decline a role without over-explaining) → writing (a short message that redesigns a plan around a stated limit). This is the pragmatics capstone of her lane: how to say a boundary kindly and how to *receive* one without pursuit.
  - **Why the real texture:** Jenny really does step back when she can't attend, always with warmth and never with drama. Modelling 裏方 as a *full* contribution teaches the learner that quiet roles count — the single most Jenny thing in the story.

- **Ch 44 (Return) — content family 43–45 (N1 editing, adaptation, mastery-aware production).**
  - **Functions:** editing/adaptation register (〜を生かして, 〜を残したまま, 省く／削る／整える), rationale-giving for an artistic constraint (〜ため, 〜ように), preserving a place without exposing content.
  - **Activity types:** reading/writing transfer — revise a public text to be vivid *without* restoring a withdrawn passage; justify the cut. Speaking support with Stasi (register comparison).
  - **Why the real texture:** **knitting = practical repair.** Re-blocking a finished piece, keeping the shape while removing a flaw, is the literal craft metaphor for N1 editing. Jenny's hands-on, quiet-competence register is the natural voice for "make it better without saying more."

- **Ch 48 (Future) — integrated N1 graduation composition (family 46–48).**
  - **Functions:** future plans **without demanding certainty** (〜と思っています, 〜かもしれない, 〜たらいいな), leaving a bounded open contribution, a final choice of *tone* rather than outcome.
  - **Activity types:** writing/speaking (produce one bounded contribution; offer a blank page under terms). Her callback line lands as *changed behaviour*, not a personalised thank-you speech.
  - **Why the real texture:** the finished-someday scarf and a plan made lightly ("〜たらいいな") are her real voice — generous, uncertain, unpressured. Japan-as-someday stays an open possibility, never a prize.

**Through-line:** Jenny is the learner's teacher for **offering, describing, and saying/hearing a soft no** — the social-glue Japanese that a grammar-first class (Henry's lane) never teaches. Her scenes make that language *necessary* because someone has genuinely gone quiet, a plan has genuinely torn, or a role genuinely needs declining.

## 4. Elective bond route — PROPOSAL (requires `bond-authored` manifest before playable)

Not yet authorised. If a reviewed manifest grants six ordered packages, this is the sketch. Each appointment carries one language function; the three required bond steps (Recognition / Friction / Support) are folded across the six. All answers are given in Japanese; approved activities only (knitting, food/bento, study-tool love-hate, music-with-Stasi, reading-the-room). Forbidden claims listed in §6.

1. **Recognition — "Count the stitches" (ordinary task).** The learner helps Jenny fix a dropped row in the perpetual scarf. *Function:* existence + counters + sensory description (何目・〜色・やわらかい). She tries it herself first, then asks one precise question — the learner sees her method before her warmth.
2. **A different route — "One gentle question first."** The learner is quiet after a hard lesson; Jenny doesn't fuss — 「今日、つかれてる？お茶、いる？」 *Function:* the ext-jenny-offer-help pattern (neutral question → offer). The learner practises offering help *and* declining it gracefully.
3. **Reciprocity — "A fun sentence, taken apart."** Jenny brings a cute bento and a ChatGPT-built "fun" sentence, then breaks it into a mini-lesson. *Function:* food vocabulary + sentence segmentation (topic/object/verb) + describing likes. Reciprocity because she teaches the way the learner earlier helped her.
4. **Friction — "Anki is giving me bombastic side eye."** Jenny confesses a study lapse and, when the learner over-helps or over-schedules, she quietly pulls back rather than argue. *Function:* expressing difficulty and frequency (〜のがなかなか…, 最近あまり…), and reading a soft withdrawal. Friction = her private-first instinct meeting someone who wants to fix her.
5. **Repair — "The back-of-house role."** Jenny names what she actually wants — 裏方, not the front. *Function:* indirect refusal + precise request (今回は…, 〜を手伝いたい), and the learner practising *hearing* a boundary (了解, じゃあ〜お願いできる？). Support step: the route redesigns around her, no pursuit.
6. **Future — "The scarf, handed on."** The finished scarf (or a plan made lightly with Stasi about a song) becomes a low-pressure future. *Function:* uncertain future (〜と思っています, 〜たらいいな) and a bounded, unpressured offer. Payoff mirrors the class-continuity `open-chair` beat without resolving the atlas.

Appointment-local callback: **the scarf** (seed A1 → friction A4 → repair/future A5–6). It cannot resolve the atlas, another person's boundary, or a lesson requirement, and the main plot keeps a fallback line for learners who never play it.
