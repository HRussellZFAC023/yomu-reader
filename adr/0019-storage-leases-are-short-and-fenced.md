# Storage leases are short where the work is storage, and fenced

GM storage has no compare-and-set, so Yomu serializes each read-modify-write of shared storage with a Storage Lease (`withGmStorageLease`): a bakery lock in which every contending tab owns one claim key, and a claim counts until its `leaseUntil`. Every lease used to last one minute and nothing told a dead holder from a slow one, so a tab that died mid-save held other tabs' saves up for about a minute behind a disabled button.

Decisions:

1. A lease whose critical section is only storage work lasts `STORAGE_WORK_LEASE_MS` (five seconds) and names the keys it guards: the local deck (`local-yomu-srs-deck`), Settings persistence (`reader-settings-persistence`) and Live Review Claims (`newtab-live-review`). A tab that dies mid-save holds the others up for five seconds at most.
2. A live holder keeps its lease. It renews on entry and on every timer tick (a third of the lease), with half a tick of slack for the time the last renewal took to land, so a tick may run about 2.3 s late. Every managed write of a guarded key also renews the lease when due, so a holder that keeps saving keeps its lease however the browser throttles its timers.
3. A holder whose lease lapsed cannot write. Other tabs treat a claim as gone at its `leaseUntil`; the holder stops writing guarded keys a landing margin (one second) before that. A lapsed claim is never written back to life: a waiter queues again, and a holder's guarded writes, rollback deletes included, are refused with `StorageLeaseLapsedError`, which managed storage passes through unwrapped. A tab that resumes with a stale snapshot therefore cannot overwrite what another tab committed meanwhile. It may leave an unreferenced card record, never a torn deck index or settings pair.
4. The cost is fail-safe. A tab suspended for more than about four seconds mid-save (an iOS app switch, a frozen background tab) has that save refused, and the learner sees the usual failure and saves again. A background tab whose timers are throttled to once a minute may re-queue on each poll and time out. A Live Review Claim refused this way asks once more instead of letting the grade through, because the claims it read may be stale; if what it read already refused the grade, that refusal stands.
5. A lease held across network requests keeps the one-minute default: the review queue (`newtab-grade-queue`, ADR-0018 decision 6) and Academy account sync. Their holders can be alive yet write nothing while they wait for a provider, and a short lease would let a second tab send the same review. The epoch-control lease keeps 30 seconds. The Japanese-site-language preference lease keeps a minute, because its section waits for the Settings lease inside it.
6. A learner's save says "Waiting for another よむ tab to finish saving…" only once another tab has held it up for about 1.5 seconds: a same-origin web lock this tab did not request, or another tab's claim ahead in the queue. Slow storage, such as Safari waking an extension's background page, and this tab's own earlier save do not count. The status clears when the save proceeds or fails.
