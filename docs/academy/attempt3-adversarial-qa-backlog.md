## Methodology & coverage

A multi-agent adversarial audit: 23 reachable screens were driven with Playwright (real onboarding click-path, no route-jumping) and each screenshot critiqued by GPT-5.5 vision (via codex, `xhigh`); in parallel, five content dimensions (living-paper visual system, story/copy, learning science, cast/consent conformance, code-safety + source-fidelity + roadmap) were audited by reading the actual source. High-severity findings then went through an adversarial "try to refute it" verification pass (skeptic + GPT-5.6-sol for visual claims). 240 raw findings were collected; refuted findings were dropped and the top tier was hand-verified by the lead before ranking.

**What this audit deliberately did _not_ do — known-and-owned, not re-reported.** `docs/academy/STATUS.md`'s gate board already documents the big structural boundaries honestly, so this audit treated them as out of scope and focused on the *quality of the reachable slice*: (a) **0 class Weeks are playable** ("grounded-playable = 0"; 72 planning-only) — a complete grounded lesson is the known missing loop; (b) the **placement mock is explicitly not a calibrated JLPT score** (it says so on-screen); (c) **Lesson-0 speech audio is 0/4 release-ready** and the release-safe audio catalogue is intentional silence (placement/lab listening use browser TTS, not source recordings); (d) only **one Moodle source question** is wired and its route is quarantined; (e) cross-device sync, mic/shadowing capture, and production art for most cast are explicit later-stage gates. None of those are reported below as defects.

**Sampling limits (be honest about these):** only the reachable slice was sampled, on a single working-tree snapshot that a concurrent session was actively editing. Deep activities (Lesson-0 kana game internals, the cafe/cafeteria/lab/street activity bodies, the bookshop) were best-effort and several were not fully entered. Only one mobile breakpoint (390×844) was checked. The full 7-step placement, the noticeboard sub-states, and the Study mount inside the Library were not exhausted. **Two auto-collected "critical" findings were harness/load artifacts, not app defects, and are excluded** (verified by the lead): the `profile-name` capture fired while the class-code gate was still submitting (caught the gate mid-`One moment…`, not Rie's name step), producing three findings that describe the gate, not the name UI; and the "two 500 errors on placement-q1" did not reproduce on a clean single-session run (the placement mock is client-side — the 500s were transient vite-dev module hiccups while ~14 capture agents hit one server during a concurrent edit). ## Forward roadmap toward N1 (building on the existing Stage 2–8 plan)

This builds on `docs/academy/STATUS.md`'s staged plan and the 73-week delivery catalogue rather than proposing a new structure. The bottleneck is not source material (a provenance-tracked census already exists: 96 Moodle archives / 688 unique payloads / a 42 GB library) — it is **delivery wiring** into playable, grounded Weeks.

- **Now → first playable Week (Stage 3 kickoff).** The highest-leverage single move is to carry *one* Week all the way through the grounding gate so `grounded-playable` goes 0 → 1. `evidence/next-grounded-week/REPORT.md` already names `l3-2-l04` as the smallest closed slice; its blockers are concrete (137 donor candidates not yet Source Questions, 114 lack loci, 28 need media review, 3 MP3s lack transcripts/timecodes). Clearing that one Week proves the whole pipeline end-to-end.
- **N5 completion.** With the pipeline proven, wire the censused Genki I / Minna I / Moodle Level-1 material into the existing 26-ish authored Weeks. Priority: reach *listening* and *speaking/output* parity — the reachable activities today are recognition-heavy (choice/cloze/match) and listening leans on TTS. Real source audio pairing (Stage 6) is the gate for N5 to feel complete.
- **N4.** Minna II ch. 24–26 bridges are already flagged as Stage-3 work; the soya corpus and Genki II cover the grammar. Focus multimodality: constructed-response (output) and kanji-production coverage should scale here.
- **N3.** This is where censused-but-unauthored material thins relative to N5/N4. The 42 GB shared library (34,222 mechanical PDF question signals, source-question review still open) is the reservoir; the risk point is that these are *mechanical candidates*, not verified Source Questions — expect the human source-question/answer/media review to be the pacing constraint, not authoring.
- **N2 → N1.** Content will run dry here unless new digitization is scheduled: the current census is strongest at N5–N4 (textbook-backed) and sparser above N3. Plan explicit acquisition/digitization of N2/N1 reading + listening source now, so the pipeline has raw material by the time authoring reaches it. The four-skill evidence model (Stage 4 banks) and recurring calibrated mock forms are the same machinery scaled up.

**Cross-cutting risks the roadmap must not paper over:** (1) delivery wiring, not content, is the true bottleneck — several fully-built modules are stranded/unwired; reconnect before rebuilding; (2) audio (source recordings, transcripts, timecodes, consent) is a standing gate at every level; (3) the answer-opacity/grounding system that makes content "provably real" is also what makes wiring expensive — keep it, but ensure the schema has data-driven fields so *content*, not *code edits*, can pass the gate.
