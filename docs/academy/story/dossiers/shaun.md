# Deep Character Dossier — Shaun

**characterId:** `shaun`
**Canonical role:** lead of `s1e22-blank-space` (Ch 22); continuity beats at 22 / 23 / 34 / 45 / 48.
**Status:** `continuity-only`. Story eligible, **lesson-ineligible**, **not bond-authored**. Cannot host a lesson or be claimed as evidence for one.
**One-line mandate (from CAST-AND-CONSENT):** *finds one story-bounded register observation, then participates without being overauthored.*

> **Author's guardrail, read this first.** Shaun is one of three people (with Mary and Takeshi) the canon explicitly names as at risk of "acquiring personality by authorial momentum." His thinness is a *design decision*, not a gap for a writer to fill. The evidence is deliberately sparse and the light voice is deliberate. Everything below is written to keep him warm and real **without** inventing a biography, a specialty, or an inner life the sources don't support. When in doubt, give the line to someone else and let Shaun stay bounded to the immediate social/register beat.

## 1. Who they really are

Shaun is the classmate who joined **after the discovery dossier had already frozen** — the person who slides into a group that has half-formed its in-jokes and just... fits, without needing a backstory reintroduction for everyone. In the real class he's a first-term face **paired with Peter**, and that pairing is the truest thing about him: two people who say little, whose few words land, and who don't compete for the floor. The canon keeps them adjacent on purpose (VOICE-AND-DIALOGUE pairs them directly).

**Likeness lock (canon, do not drift):** light-brown layered hair, round glasses, a warm smile, a beige zip fleece, dark trousers, backpack straps. His approved v002 family carries all seven core performances in that same outfit; the pastry bag and folded cue page appear only where those immediate scene beats call for them.

His comedy is physical and gentle: **he arrives late, and a pastry collapses.** That's the whole gag and it's enough — the croissant/pastry that doesn't survive the journey, produced apologetically as he takes the last chair a beat after the scene has started. It reads as endearing, never as irresponsibility. In a class where **lateness is the group's signature comedic engine** ("Late is just a mindset"), Shaun is the quiet variation on the theme: not defiantly late like the running bit, just... *arrives*, slightly rumpled, bearing a snack that has seen better days.

**Reconciling with the voice anchor.** The anchor says: *deliberately light voice; not over-authored; his few words must not imply a hidden biography* (VOICE-AND-DIALOGUE, Peter/Shaun row). Where Peter reframes a task through a **question**, Shaun stays **bounded to the immediate social or register beat** — he notices how something *sounds*, right now, in the room, and says the small true thing about it. He is not the class's grammarian, not its calibration voice (that's Sophie), not its form-decomposer (that's Shin). He's the person who hears when spoken Japanese has gone stiff and unnatural, and points at it once.

**What we do NOT assign him:** no job, no hobby-specialty, no learning-tool obsession, no "lane." The wider class's rich texture — jpdb curation, Anki grinding, Duolingo streaks, immersion foodie-ism, the never-happening karaoke thread — belongs to the named leads (Henry, Tom, Aakash, and the ensemble). Shaun can *stand near* those jokes and enjoy them; he does not own one. That restraint is the characterization.

## 2. Four-season arc (his 5 continuity beats)

Shaun's deeper arc is the smallest and most honest in the cast: **a late arrival discovers he has one genuinely useful thing to say, says it, and then earns the right to simply belong** — without the story ever inflating that one contribution into a personality. His beats span Seasons 2–4.

**Arrival — Ch 22 `s1e22-blank-space` (his lead).**
The class's rebuilt route dialogue (the atlas's in-game NPC lines) **stays unnaturally formal under pressure** — every character keeps speaking rigid です/ます even when the moment is casual, tense, or intimate, and it sounds wrong. Shaun comes in **late, pastry collapsing**, takes the open chair, and — because he's been half-listening from the doorway rather than deep in the fix — hears the thing the busy people stopped hearing: *"...they'd talk like that in a manual. Not like that to a friend."* That's his one story-bounded register observation. His **bounded contribution** is to flag the mismatch, not to solve the whole script. The blank space on the board gets one honest note in it: real people shift register. (Callback seed: `callback:register-shift`, below.)

**Contribution — Ch 23 `s1e23-farewell-rehearsal`.**
Nanako's guide role is ending and the planned farewell meal falls apart; the class rehearses gratitude and intentions. Shaun's register ear does one small useful job here: he catches where a rehearsed thank-you has gone **too formal to feel true**, or too casual to feel respectful, and nudges one line back into the right register. He does not lead the farewell — he tunes one sentence of it. (Echo of the register seed, in service of someone else's scene.)

**Limit — Ch 34 `s3e10-empty-microphone`.**
A host drops out; the group must redistribute public speaking **without coercion**. This is Shaun's boundary beat, and it's the most important one for keeping him honest: when the microphone comes toward him, **Shaun's limit is that he does not want the public-speaking role** — and the class lets a quiet role count as full participation. His refusal is not a confidence deficit to be cured; it's a legitimate shape of belonging. He can still do the register-check work *backstage* (catching a stiff line before it goes live) without standing at the mic. (This is the canon's "quiet roles count as full participation" applied to the one person built for exactly that lesson.)

**Return — Ch 45 `s4e09-rehearsal-for-leaving`.**
Relationship callbacks surface as **practical acts** while the class rehearses both the event and the goodbye. Shaun's return proves the ensemble heard his Ch 34 limit: nobody hands him a microphone this time. Instead his register-check is *asked for* as the backstage job it always was — support shown before it's named. His late-and-pastry entrance can pay off warmly here as familiarity, not chaos: the class now expects him a beat late and saves him the chair.

**Future — Ch 48 `s4e12-next-page`.**
At graduation, Shaun's future beat leaves **one next possibility open without predicting his real life.** In the controlled montage he is one of the selected callbacks (or recorded in the journal without a spoken roll-call line — the canon allows either). The right note: the person who arrived after the dossier froze, who once only had one line to offer, is now unremarkably, fully part of the class. The blank page stays blank; he doesn't have to fill it to have belonged.

## 3. The Japanese their story teaches (LEARNING INTEGRATION)

> **Hard constraint:** Shaun is **lesson-ineligible**. He cannot *host* a registered lesson or be cited as its evidence (CAST-AND-CONSENT Ch-22 row: "cannot host or be claimed as evidence for a lesson"). But his **lead chapter 22 still names language functions** that the registered reservoir satisfies, and his register observation is the *narrative reason* the learner needs that language. The lesson is owned by the registered package; Shaun's scene owns the **need**. Keep that seam clean: Shaun creates the reason, the package teaches and checks.

**His learning speciality (story-side only): register.** The single legitimate thing Shaun's presence surfaces is the **casual/formal register contrast** — plain form vs. です/ます, and the way spoken Japanese *shifts* depending on who's talking to whom and under what pressure. This is his only teaching function and it appears **in story only** (CAST-AND-CONSENT, VOICE-AND-DIALOGUE both state this explicitly). He is not a grammar lane; he is one register observation made human.

### Lead chapter — Ch 22 `s1e22-blank-space`

**Registered reservoir (CONTENT-LINKAGE):** `l2-l23`–`l2-l25` — *use kanji in a useful message, reopen a conversation, and give advice with room to decline.*

**Transfer function the scene makes the learner NEED:**

| Language function | Activity type | How Shaun's real texture makes it necessary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Register shift: plain form ⇄ です/ます** in dialogue | **Reading + speaking (production/transformation)** | Shaun *hears* the NPC lines sound like a manual, not a friend. The learner's task becomes: take a stiff formal game line and re-voice it in the register a real friend would use (and vice-versa where deference is right). The comedy of "a pastry-bearing latecomer notices the robots talk weird" is the mnemonic hook. |

**Beat pattern (per CONTENT-LINKAGE §"Story-to-lesson beat pattern"):**
1. **Need** — the rebuilt route dialogue sounds inhumanly formal; it breaks immersion; someone has to hear *why*.
2. **Input** — Shaun, from the doorway, voices the mismatch; the learner sees a stiff です/ます line beside how a friend would actually say it.
3. **Lesson** — the registered `l2-l23`–`l2-l25` activity teaches and checks register-shift, re-entry, and advice-with-decline. **Shaun is not the evidence; the package is.**

**Downstream learning beats (support, not lead):**
- **Ch 23** — register tuning of a rehearsed gratitude line (intentions/before-after/gratitude reservoir owned by Nanako's chapter). Shaun corrects register; he does not host.
- **Ch 34** — the **refusal** itself is the language object: declining the public role *politely and firmly* (N3 refusal / role negotiation, `28–30` required family). Shaun's "no" models a calibrated, non-coerced decline — a full-skill speaking act.
- **Ch 45** — mastery-aware production: his register-check is now a fluent, unremarked backstage skill (support shown before named).
- **Ch 48** — graduation bounded contribution: a final small register-appropriate line, or a journal entry.

## 4. Elective bond route

**Shaun is NOT bond-authored.** The RELATIONSHIP-MATRIX consent note reads *"story-only; no lesson ownership or invented specialty,"* and CAST-AND-CONSENT records his bond column as **no**. Elective appointments require a separate reviewed manifest entry with state `bond-authored`; **no status in the matrix grants one, and Shaun has not been granted one.**

**Therefore this dossier deliberately does NOT sketch a six-appointment bond route.** Authoring one would violate the exact failure mode the canon names for him: acquiring personality (interests, private history, a specialty) by authorial momentum. A Persona/Fire-Emblem support chain would force invented hobbies and disclosures the sources don't support.

**If a bond route is ever authorized in future,** these are the non-negotiable guardrails for whoever writes it:
- It may only build on his **one canon competence** (register ear) and his **one canon comedy** (late + pastry) — no new job, no new hobby, no backstory.
- Recognition / Friction / Support steps must stay **bounded to immediate social/register beats**; friction cannot become a personal-history reveal.
- Any appointment-local payoff cannot resolve the atlas, another person's boundary, or a lesson requirement (per CALLBACK-LEDGER relationship-callback rules).
- Reconfirm consent state before writing a single line; the default remains `continuity-only`.

Until then: Shaun's belonging is proven through the **class-continuity montage**, not an elective route. That is by design and it is enough.
